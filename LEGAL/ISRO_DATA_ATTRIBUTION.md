# ISRO / Chandrayaan-2 Data Attribution

## Two separate things

This project consists of two clearly separate things, and this separation is maintained
throughout all CHANDRABHAR-2 documentation:

> **"Software developed as CHANDRABHAR-2"** — an independent research prototype, not affiliated
> with or endorsed by ISRO.

versus

> **"Chandrayaan-2 mission data supplied/archived by the relevant official ISRO/PRADAN
> infrastructure"** — owned and governed entirely by ISRO/ISSDC, subject to their own terms.

## What CHANDRABHAR-2 is not

- CHANDRABHAR-2 is **not** an official ISRO software product.
- CHANDRABHAR-2 is **not** ISRO-certified, ISRO-approved, or ISRO-endorsed in any capacity.
- CHANDRABHAR-2 does **not** claim any ownership interest in, or exclusive rights to,
  Chandrayaan-2 mission data.
- The name "CHANDRABHAR-2" does not imply affiliation with the Chandrayaan-2 mission itself
  beyond processing its publicly-archived data products, and does not imply this software is a
  second/companion mission or spacecraft.

## Attribution

Chandrayaan-2 OHRC, TMC-2, and IIRS products referenced or processed by this software originate
from the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO), distributed via the PRADAN archive operated
by the Indian Space Science Data Centre (ISSDC): https://pradan.issdc.gov.in/ch2/. Verify current
attribution and citation requirements at that source before publishing results.
