# CHANDRABHAR-2 — Brand Guidelines

## Name

**CHANDRABHAR-2** — always written in this exact casing as the product/project name.
("Chandrabhar" combines *Chandra* (moon) with *Bhar*/reference to correspondence-bearing across
the two Chandrayaan-2 look angles; -2 ties it to the Chandrayaan-2 mission it processes data
from.) The name does **not** imply this is a second ISRO mission, a successor spacecraft, or an
ISRO product line — it is the name of this independent research-prototype software.

## Logo

- The supplied `logo.png` / `logo_transparent.png` is the single official mark. Do not redraw,
  recolor, distort, or reconstruct it.
- Minimum clear space: 0.5× the logo's own height on all sides.
- Do not place the logo on busy photographic backgrounds; use Mission Black or Panel Black.
- Do not rotate, skew, add drop shadows, or apply filters to the logo.
- Favicon/app-icon crops (`favicon.png`, `app_icon.png`) are square center-crops of the same
  mark for platforms that require a square asset (browser tab, taskbar) — no redesign.

## Tone of voice

- Technical, precise, restrained. No hype adjectives ("revolutionary", "breakthrough",
  "state-of-the-art") unless a specific measured result supports the claim.
- Always distinguish *software correctness* (tests passing, code running) from *scientific
  validation* (the evidence gate on real mission data). Never conflate the two in copy.
- Never imply ISRO endorsement, certification, or affiliation. See
  `LEGAL/ISRO_DATA_ATTRIBUTION.md` and `LEGAL/SOFTWARE_DISCLAIMER.md`.

## Disallowed phrasing

Do not use, anywhere in product copy, docs, or presentation material, unless independently and
currently supported by an actual passing evidence-gate result on real data:

- "solves the Chandrayaan-2 correspondence problem"
- "ISRO-certified" / "ISRO-approved"
- "mission-ready"
- "subpixel accurate" (unless a specific subpixel benchmark is cited)
- "fully validated"

## Approved subtitle

> TMC-2 and IIRS imagery
